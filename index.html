Yangbo Ye

Postdoctoral Researcher · Department of Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences, Fudan University

I am a postdoctoral researcher studying extreme weather and climate in the Department of Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences at Fudan University, working with Prof. Wen Zhou.

My research focuses on weather and climate extremes in a changing climate, including event attribution and impact attribution. I study a range of extremes, from extreme cold, heatwaves, and heavy rainfall to spatially compound extreme events.

  • Compound extreme events
  • Climate change
  • Extreme-event attribution
