Doctoral research: extreme-event attribution

  • Studied extreme cold, heat, precipitation, and compound events in China using observations, ERA5, HadGEM3-A-N216, and CMIP6, including projections of future risks.
  • Developed a rapid extreme-event attribution system and contributed to communicating research findings to the public.
